THE PROBLEM

Too Much Food. Too Little Coordination.

Institutional kitchens prepare thousands of meals every day, but unpredictable demand often results in edible food being wasted.

🍲

Overproduction

Kitchens often prepare more food than actually required because future consumption is difficult to predict.

🗑️

Food Waste

Large quantities of perfectly edible food may end up as waste after meal services.

🔗

No Coordination

NGOs and food banks may need food, but institutions lack a fast and reliable redistribution network.

🌍

Environmental Impact

Wasted food also means wasted water, energy, money and unnecessary environmental emissions.

OUR SOLUTION
